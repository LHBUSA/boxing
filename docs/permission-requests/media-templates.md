# Media permission request templates: DRAFTS, NOT SENT

Status (2026-09-14): reusable templates for owner review. **Nothing has been sent, and nothing here is permission.**
A grant only counts once it is stored (PDF or email export, outside Git) and recorded as a review decision
(`permission_granted` with a `permission_reference` in `boxing_person_media` / `boxing_organization_media`, or a
new portrait registry entry with rule `permission`). A "no" or silence changes nothing.

Every request asks for **editorial identification only**: showing a photo on PropBetEdge pages about that boxer,
person or organization. Never advertising, endorsement, affiliation, share images or resale. Credit exactly as the
rights holder specifies. Images are stored by PropBetEdge (no hotlinking) and removed on request.

Placeholders: `{sender_name}`, `{sender_title}`, `{reply_address}`, `{subject_name}`, `{assets}` (file names or URLs),
`{pages}` (example PropBetEdge URLs once public), `{credit_line}`.

Priority targets come from `reviews/media/2026-09-14-portraits.md` ("identity proven, no usable Commons image"):
fighters on upcoming and recent main events first, then title fighters, then fighters with several verified bouts.

any affiliation with your company.

If you agree, a short written reply naming the photos (or "all headshots in your media kit") and the credit line is
enough. If you would rather not, no problem: we show a neutral graphic instead.

Thank you,
{sender_name} · {reply_address}
